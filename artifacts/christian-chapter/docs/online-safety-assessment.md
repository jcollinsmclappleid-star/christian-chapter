# Online safety — short assessment

Internal. 4 October 2026. Ianson Systems Limited. This is not an Ofcom filing and not a claim that the service is safe.

The service is user-to-user only after staff connect two members. There is no open browse of members and no public profile index.

Risks that fit that shape: harassment, hate, sexual content, romance fraud, and an adult contacting someone who lied about their age. Likelihood is limited while matching is not open to everyone. Harm is serious if a private message or a faith profile is misused.

Controls in the product: email confirmation, age 40 or over from the date of birth given, profanity blocked on names and profiles, a block that closes the thread, a report that keeps only the messages the member selects for 90 days, and ordinary messages deleted after 7 days from first read or 30 days if unread.

Still open: no multi-factor on the admin password, no check of age beyond the date of birth the person types, and no written Ofcom category. Review this note before messaging is opened to members generally.
