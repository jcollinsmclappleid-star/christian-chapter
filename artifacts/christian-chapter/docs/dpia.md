# Data protection impact assessment

Internal. Not a public page. Controller: Ianson Systems Limited (17084981, ICO ZC127831). The company is not VAT registered.

Date: 4 October 2026. Owner: the director responsible for Mature Christian Dating. Review before matching, member messaging, or billing is switched on, or if we start using a new provider that sees profile or faith data.

## Why this exists

We collect religious belief, which is special-category data, and we can show a profile and a photograph to another adult. That is enough to write this down. The service is still a founding cohort: no open marketplace, no card taken, no automated match.

## What we need it for

Adults aged 40 and over write a profile so a matchmaker can consider one introduction. Faith is part of that profile. We cannot do the stated job without the faith answers, and we do not take them without a separate checkbox. Date of birth is used only to check the age rule. We do not take a street address.

## Risks that matter

| Risk | What we do about it |
|---|---|
| Faith or a profile is seen by someone who should not see it | Staff and matchmakers only, until we choose an introduction. Another member sees a photograph only after a person approves it. Approval is not an identity check |
| Someone feels they must tick the faith box | The checkbox is separate. If they withdraw consent, faith processing stops and the dating application closes. The site says that before they tick |
| A member is not who they claim, or asks for money | We do not promise a background check. Terms say not to send money. We can refuse or close an account. Block and report exist where two members are in the product. A block stops a new message |
| A check photograph leaks | Kept only until the check finishes or 24 hours, not sent to another company, deleted at once on closure |
| Messages kept too long | Unread messages expire after 30 days. The other person's first read starts a 7-day clock. Either account closing deletes the thread. There is no separate copy kept for a report yet |
| Account closure is slow | Profile hidden immediately. Deletion after 30 days. Backups are not instant |
| A provider is breached | Hashed tokens, no card data. We tell the person and the ICO when the law requires it |

## What we will not claim

We do not claim 24-hour moderation, identity verification, or that a match will happen. Matching technology and billing stay off until we review this note again.

## Decision

Proceed with the founding cohort on the controls above. Do not switch on member-wide messaging, automated matching, or card payments without a short addition to this note and to `docs/record-of-processing.md`.
