# Children's access — short assessment

Internal. 4 October 2026. Ianson Systems Limited. This is not a finding that a child cannot reach the site.

The service is written for adults aged 40 and over. It is not aimed at children. A person cannot submit an application unless the date of birth they enter makes them 40 or over. That is a declaration, not a check of an identity document.

A child who types an adult date of birth would not be stopped by that gate. If we learn an account is being used by someone under 18, we close it and delete what we are not required to keep. There is no separate child account and no part of the service designed for under-18s.

Review this if the age rule changes or if messaging is opened without a further age check.
