## Colours
- `--color-ivory: #F7F3EC` — page background
- `--color-plum: #1E1220` — primary text
- `--color-oxblood: #8B1F2F` — primary CTA, accent
- `--color-evergreen: #1D4A36` — trust/safety sections
- `--color-brass: #C4A05A` — sparingly, decorative
- `--color-stone: #9A8E9A` — captions, secondary labels

## Typography
- Serif (display): EB Garamond, `font-family-serif`, `--font-garamond`
- Sans (UI): Space Grotesk, `font-family-sans`, `--font-grotesk`
- Body min 17px desktop; min 44px touch targets

## Button variants
- `primary` — oxblood bg, ivory text
- `ghost` — plum border, plum text
- `trust` — evergreen bg, ivory text
- `outline` — oxblood border, oxblood text

**Why:** Brand positions as warm, considered, editorial — not clinical or swipe-heavy. Plum + ivory = premium print editorial; oxblood = warm urgency without aggression.
